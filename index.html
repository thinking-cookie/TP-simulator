How to drive
W↑
Accelerator (moves you in the gear shown)
S↓
Brake (never changes gear)
RF
Shift to Reverse / Drive (car must be stopped)
AD
Steer left / right
Space
Handbrake (hold)
Shift
Manual: clutch. Hold to press it down, let go to lift it slowly
Shift↑↓
Manual: with the clutch down, shift up / down (R N 1 2 3 4 5)
VI
Manual: hold clutch at biting point / start engine
⌫
Retry latest checkpoint (pick others with the Checkpoints button)
ZX
Left / right signal (turns off after the turn)
QE
Check left / right blind spot. Hold to look over your shoulder
C
Switch view: car-up, north-up, driver's seat
B
Driver's seat: 360° camera on the dash screen on / off
T
CDC map: other cars and people on / off
G
Beginner guide on / off (driver's seat): when to get ready, where to stop, parking sensor beeps
Follow the yellow arrow. Before turning inside a blue dashed zone, signal (Z/X) and check your blind spot (Q/E). On the CDC map, stop at red lights and STOP lines and give way at the zebra.
